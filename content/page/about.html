About FNVA

The Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives is a not-for-profit, non-partisan organisation working on Tibet, China and the Himalayan frontier from an Indian policy perspective.

FNVA is dedicated to fostering a nuanced and policy-relevant understanding of the PRC, Tibet and the Himalayan frontier region from an Indian strategic perspective.

Established in 2009, the Foundation describes itself as the sole Indian think-tank specialising on Tibet. Its work is rooted in the principles of non-violence, human dignity and the rule of law, and it serves as a platform for informed dialogue, interdisciplinary research and public engagement on the regional dynamics that bear directly on India's national interest and the wider Asian landscape.

Tibet significantly influences India's border security, ecological balance, water systems and strategic calculus with China. FNVA exists in large part to address the knowledge gap on these questions in India's policy discourse.

TransHimalaya

TransHimalaya is FNVA's quarterly magazine of ideas, policy and perspectives. It carries research essays, commentary, field reports and interviews on Tibet, the Himalaya and Inner Asia, written by scholars, journalists and practitioners from across the region and beyond.
