Kate Saunders

Tibet specialist

Kate Saunders is a Tibet specialist, journalist and author currently working with Tibetan researchers for Tibet Watch in the UK, a leading source of news and analysis on Tibet. Kate is the author of 'Eighteen Layers of Hell: Stories from the Chinese Gulag' and has written for publications worldwide including the London Times, Guardian and Washington Post.
