Rinzin Namgyal

Research Associate at the Foundation for Non-Violent Alternatives (FNVA)

Rinzin Namgyal is a Research Associate at the Foundation for Non-Violent Alternatives (FNVA) and a NASC (2025–26) Fellow at Takshashila Institution. He is pursuing a PhD in Chinese Studies at the University of Delhi, where he completed his Master's in East Asian Studies specialising in China. His research focuses on Chinese domestic politics, critical infrastructure, security studies, Sino–Pakistan relations, and Tibet–China relations.
