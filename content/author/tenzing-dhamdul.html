Tenzing Dhamdul

Senior Researcher at the Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives (FNVA)

Tenzing Dhamdul is Senior Researcher at the Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives (FNVA), and researches on Tibetan Affairs with focus on Tibet, India, and China relations. He is a recipient of the Sikyong Scholarship 2015, and has completed his Under Graduation and Post Graduation from Delhi University’s Hindu College and Jawaharlal Nehru University, respectively. His has published with FNVA and Springer, and has written for Caravan Magazine, the Taipei Times, India’s World, the Diplomat, Domino Theory, and the Taiwan Times, etc. Dhamdul has appeared on several interviews and podcasts, and also hosts FNVA’s Podcast series ‘Unsilenced: Voices of Young Tibetans’.
