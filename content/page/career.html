Career & Contribute

Write for us, apply for an internship, or get in touch about working with the Foundation.

Write for TransHimalaya

We welcome critical dialogue on developments in China, the Tibet Plateau and the Himalayan frontier regions — including Mongolia and Myanmar — and their implications for India's security. That includes the environment and climate change, energy security, political transitions and human security. Other subjects may be considered; write to us and ask.

In-text references are required for the longer formats. The Foundation provides an honorarium for all finalised submissions — within five working days for commentaries, and within two weeks for longer pieces.

  • Commentary800–850 wordsShort pieces on current issues.
  • Issue Brief2,500–2,800 wordsA detailed description of a topical subject.
  • Special Reportaround 5,000 wordsIn-depth analysis.
  • Research Essay10,000+ wordsExtended scholarly work.
  • Book Review1,000–1,500 wordsReviews of new writing on the region.

Internships

FNVA runs an internship programme for students and early-career researchers working on China, Tibet and the Himalaya. Interns contribute to research, translation and events, and are supervised by the research team in New Delhi.

How to apply

Send submissions, internship applications and general enquiries to office@fnvaworld.org, or use the contact form. Please include a short note on your background and, for written work, an outline or draft.
