Biswas Baral

Editor of The Kathmandu Post

Biswas Baral is the Editor of The Kathmandu Post and a columnist for The Diplomat. With two decades of experience in Nepali journalism, he previously served as the editor-in-chief of The Annapurna Express, a national news weekly, and is host of the popular YouTube show ‘Idea of Nepal’. He mostly writes on Nepal’s domestic politics and its foreign policy. Baral holds a Master’s Degree in Political Science from the Tribhuvan University and an Executive MBA from the Ace Institute of Management.
