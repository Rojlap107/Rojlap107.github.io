International Delegations Visit Dharamshala

February 2026

Taiwanese Delegation Visits Tibetan Parliament

A Taiwanese legislative delegation led by Wu Pei-yi, President of the Taiwan Parliamentary Group for Tibet, visited the Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile on 26 February, meeting the Speaker and Deputy Speaker to discuss issues of mutual interest. The delegation also included Fa Hui Shen, Member of the Legislative Yuan; Chen Chang Lee and Miao Po-ya, Taipei City Councilors; and Tsuni Cheng, Vice President of the Taiwan Foundation for Democracy.

March 2026

Central Tibetan Administration Commemorates 67th Tibetan National Uprising Day

The CTA observed the 67th anniversary of the Tibetan National Uprising Day at the Tsuglagkhang courtyard, marking over six decades of resistance against the occupation of Tibet. Chief guests included Prof. Dr Hans-Gert Pöttering, former President of the European Parliament; Senator Jiří Oberfalzer, Vice-President of the Czech Senate; and Michael Brand MP, German Parliamentary State Secretary, joined by Brian Joseph of the National Endowment for Democracy, journalist Cheng Lei, and other international guests.

March 2026

US–China Economic and Security Review Commission Meets Tibetan Parliament’s Speaker and Deputy Speaker

The delegation included Michael Kuiken, Vice Chair for the 2026 Report Cycle; Commissioners Reva Price and Taylor Budowich; Kathleen Hawk, Director of Security and Foreign Affairs; and officials from the US Embassy in New Delhi.

May 2026

UK Parliamentary Delegation Visits Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile

Labour MP Kerry McCarthy and Liberal Democrat MP Wera Hobhouse visited the Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile, attending the swearing-in ceremony of Sikyong Penpa Tsering.

Happenings in Dharamshala

June 2026

Six Kalons of the 17th Kashag Sworn In

The elected ministers — Tsegyal Chukya Dranyi (Kashag Secretary), Palden Dhondup (Home), Tsering Dhondup Gyangser (Finance), Ugyen Tenzin, Kunga Tashi and Pema Tso — took the oath of office before Yeshi Wangmo, Chief Justice of the Tibetan Supreme Justice Commission.

Photo: Tenzin Jigme Taydeh / CTA

June 2026

Members of the 18th Parliament-in-Exile Sworn In

The newly elected members of the 18th Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile were sworn in at the Parliament Hall in the presence of Sikyong Penpa Tsering, in accordance with the Charter of Tibetans in Exile. Speaker-elect Dolma Tsering Teykhang and Deputy Speaker-elect Khenpo Sonam Tenphel took their oaths from Chief Justice Commissioner Yeshi Wangmo — Dolma Tsering Teykhang becoming the first woman to serve as Speaker of the Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile, having previously served as Deputy Speaker of the 17th Parliament. Khenpo Sonam Tenphel begins his second term as Deputy Speaker, having previously served as Speaker of the 16th and 17th Parliaments.

June 2026

Penpa Tsering Sworn In for Second Term as Sikyong

Penpa Tsering was sworn in as Sikyong of the 17th Kashag in the presence of His Holiness the Dalai Lama, following his re-election as the 6th directly elected Sikyong. The ceremony was attended by a cross-party UK parliamentary delegation, parliamentarians from Chile and El Salvador, and Indian representatives including Lok Sabha MP Tapir Gao and Rajya Sabha MP Sujeet Kumar.

March 2026

More Than 100 Members of Tibet Support Groups Gather for Special International Meeting

Following three days of deliberation on the situation inside Tibet, strategies to counter China’s narratives, and why Tibet matters in the evolving geopolitical landscape, the Special International Tibet Support Groups Meeting concluded on the eve of the 67th anniversary of Tibetan National Uprising Day.

March 2026

The 8th All India Tibet Support Groups Conference Held in Dharamshala

The conference reaffirmed solidarity with the Tibetan people, expressed concern over human rights violations and environmental degradation in Tibet, called for renewed dialogue and recognition of the CTA, rejected Chinese interference in the Dalai Lama’s reincarnation, and resolved to strengthen Tibet advocacy across India through public outreach and youth engagement.

Photo: CTA

March 2026

His Holiness Attends Long Life Prayers Offered by the All-India Tibet Support Groups

As part of celebrations marking his 90th birthday and the Year of Compassion, more than 400 members of the All-India Tibet Support Groups presented His Holiness with the Karuna Ratna — the Jewel of Compassion, a golden bodhi-leaf bearing the Dharma wheel and teaching mudra — honouring his lifelong dedication to peace and compassion, and reaffirming support for his Four Noble Commitments and Tibet’s freedom.

Photo: dalailama.com

July 2026

His Holiness Celebrates His 91st Birthday in Leh

His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama celebrated his 91st birthday on 6 July 2026 with a grand ceremony at the Shewatsel Teaching Ground in Leh, Ladakh, attended by an estimated 25,000 devotees, with messages of goodwill arriving from India, North America and Europe. Reflecting on the day, His Holiness said: “I’m 91, but according to indications in my dreams it seems I may yet live to be 130. So, I hope to be able to help Chinese people through the teaching of the Buddha, and also to help people around the world to lead good, positive lives. This is what I aspire to do — thank you.”

International Developments & Parliamentary Engagements

February 2026

Australian APPG Meets on Tibet

The Australian All-Party Parliamentary Group for Tibet met during parliament’s Autumn sitting to chalk out action plans. Senator Barbara Pocock, who recently visited Dharamshala, shared firsthand insights on Tibetan leadership and refugee communities.

March 2026

Australia and New Zealand Hold 2+2 Ministerial Meeting

At their third Foreign and Defence Ministerial Consultation in Canberra on 17 March, Australia and New Zealand expressed serious concerns about human rights issues in Tibet, Xinjiang and Hong Kong under CCP rule.

April 2026

Czech Senate Passes Historic Tibet Resolution

On 10 April, the Czech Senate passed a resolution directly challenging China’s interference in the Dalai Lama’s succession — one of the most direct European legislative stances on the succession issue to date.

Senator Jitka Svietlova, Vice-President of the Czech Senate, presenting the resolution on 25 March. Photo: Czech Senate webcast

May 2026

European Parliament Condemns China’s Ethnic Unity Law

The European Parliament strongly condemned China’s Ethnic Unity Law, called for its repeal and sanctions, and held a Human Rights Subcommittee hearing at which the ICT President urged member states toward strong, coordinated action.

May 2026

New US Bipartisan Legislation on the CTA

Congressmen Michael McCaul (R-TX) and Jim McGovern (D-MA) introduced legislation seeking to strengthen America’s formal relationship with the Central Tibetan Administration.

1 May 2026

US Senate Bill on China’s Atrocities in Tibet

Senators Jeff Merkley and Rick Scott introduced a bipartisan bill requiring the State Department to formally examine China’s atrocities in Tibet — going a step beyond the existing Resolve Tibet Act.

Other News

March 2026

Tsering Dhongthog Becomes First Tibetan-American Deputy Assistant Secretary at the US State Department

Tsering Dhongthog was appointed Deputy Assistant Secretary for Trade Policy and Negotiations at the US Department of State, marking a historic milestone as the first Tibetan-American to hold a senior position within the department.

April–May 2026

37th Birthday of the 11th Panchen Lama, and 31 Years of Disappearance

The CTA marked the 37th birthday of Gedhun Choekyi Nyima with a month-long awareness campaign, including a pictorial exhibition in Dharamshala, official birthday celebrations at Tashi Lhunpo Monastery in Bylakuppe, and awareness sessions across Tibetan communities globally.

24 May 2026

Sikyong Attends US Independence Day Reception in Delhi

At the invitation of US Ambassador Sergio Gor, Sikyong Penpa Tsering, along with Kalon Norzin Dolma and Representative Jigme Jugney, attended the Freedom 250 Independence Day Reception in Delhi alongside delegates from various countries.

1 June 2026

Tibetan Activist Released but Remains Under Restrictions

Environmental defender and anti-corruption whistleblower Anya Sengdra was released from Mianyang Prison on 7 February 2026. The Chinese government continues to restrict his movements, conduct arbitrary phone searches and interrogations, and deny him the right to seek medical treatment without prior government permission.

6 June 2026

Dalai Lama Departs Dharamshala for Medical Treatment

His Holiness the Dalai Lama departed Dharamshala for Delhi to undergo medical treatment on his left knee. Following recovery, he was expected to travel to Ladakh toward the end of June for an extended stay.

2 July 2026

Tibetan Activist Self-immolates Outside the UN in New York

Lobga Rangzen (also known as Lobsang Palden) self-immolated outside the United Nations headquarters in New York, a day after China’s “Law on Promoting Ethnic Unity and Progress” went into effect. He is the first Tibetan to self-immolate in the United States, and the 170th to carry out this form of protest — of which 159 have occurred inside Tibet, and 11 in exile, primarily in India and Nepal.

Photo: Lobga Rangzen / Facebook / Reuters
