Patron

Ven. Samdhong Lobsang Tenzin Rinpoche

Former Kalon Tripa, Central Tibetan Administration

A Tibetan Buddhist monk, scholar and statesman, Samdhong Rinpoche was the first directly elected Kalon Tripa — the political head of the Central Tibetan Administration — serving from 2001 to 2011. A lifelong exponent of Gandhian non-violence and Buddhist philosophy, he has taught and written widely on both.
