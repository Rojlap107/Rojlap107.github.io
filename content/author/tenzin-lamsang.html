Tenzin Lamsang

Founder

Tenzing Lamsang is the founder and editor of The Bhutanese newspaper. He is an award winning journalist from Bhutan and brings a wealth of critical perspectives and a deeper understanding of the daily issues affecting the nation. He is best known for his daring investigative journalism.
