Shyam Saran

Former Foreign Secretary of India

Ambassador Shyam Saran is a former Foreign Secretary of India and former Chairman of the National Security Advisory Board. He is President of the India International Centre and a Trustee of the Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives (FNVA), and contributed the Foreword to the inaugural issue of TransHimalaya.
