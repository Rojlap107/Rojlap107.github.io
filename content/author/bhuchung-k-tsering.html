Bhuchung K Tsering

Research & Monitoring Unit

Bhuchung Tsering heads the Research & Monitoring Unit, International Campaign for Tibet, Washington D.C. He was a member of the Task Force set up by the Central Tibetan Administration to work on issues relating to the dialogue process and was also a member of the ream led by the envoys of the Dalai Lama in the discussions with the Chinese leadership between 2002 and 2010.
