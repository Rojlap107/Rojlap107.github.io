Tenzin Dalha

Research Fellow at the Tibet Policy Institute

Tenzin Dalha is a Research Fellow at the Tibet Policy Institute, Central Tibetan Administration, where he researches the impact of social media on Tibetan communities in Tibet and exile, as well as Chinese cyber security policy. He received his M.Phil. in Journalism and Communication from the University of Madras in 2014. He has presented at institutions including Johns Hopkins University and the University of Westminster, and has contributed commentaries to The Diplomat and Global-is-Asian. Prior to joining TPI, he worked as a freelance journalist and documentary contributor.
