About TransHimalaya

An independent, interdisciplinary research and policy journal examining the forces and transformations shaping Tibet and Asia’s upland regions.

Anchored in the Himalayan–Tibetan continuum and extending across Inner and Central Asia, it brings together rigorous scholarship, policy-relevant analysis and regional voices. Published by the Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives.
