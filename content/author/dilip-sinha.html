Dilip Sinha

A writer

Dilip Sinha, a former diplomat, is now a writer and public speaker. He was India's ambassador to the United Nations in Geneva and to Greece, and head of the International Organisations and the Pakistan-Afghanistan-Iran divisions in the Ministry of External Affairs. He has authored two books: (i) Imperial Games in Tibet: The Struggle for Statehood and Sovereignty, and (ii) Legitimacy of Power: The Permanence of Five in the Security Council. 1: Jawaharlal Nehru, 'Statement in Parliament on 4 September 1959', in India's Foreign Policy, New Delhi, Publications Division, Government of India, 1961, p. 344.
