Frequently Asked Questions

How to cite TransHimalaya, how copyright works, and how footnotes should be written.

How should I cite an article from TransHimalaya?

Use the following format:

Author’s Name, “Article Title,” TransHimalaya: Strategy, Culture and Change in Tibet and Asia’s Uplands, Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives (FNVA), New Delhi, Vol. X, YEAR, pp. xx–xx. DOI.

How does copyright work for content published in TransHimalaya?

All content published in TransHimalaya is protected by copyright. How should footnotes be written?

Footnotes are numbered in the order they appear in the text, marked with a superscript numeral, and listed in full at the end of the article. Each footnote should give the full source on first citation — author, title, publisher, place and year of publication, and page number — and may be shortened on later reference to the same source. Each entry links back to the point in the text where it is cited.

Who is responsible for the views expressed in an article?

The views expressed in individual articles are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Editorial Board nor the Foundation for Non-violent Alternatives (FNVA).
