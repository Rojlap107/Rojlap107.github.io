Trustee

Amb. Shyam Saran

Former Foreign Secretary, Government of India

A career diplomat, Shyam Saran served as Foreign Secretary of India from 2004 to 2006, and subsequently as the Prime Minister’s Special Envoy on the civil nuclear agreement and on climate change. He has since chaired and advised a number of institutions on foreign affairs and strategic questions.
