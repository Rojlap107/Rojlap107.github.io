Bhuchung D Sonam

Exiled Tibetan writer

Bhuchung D Sonam is an exiled Tibetan writer, translator, and publisher whose works explore contemporary Tibetan literature, identity, politics, music, and culture. His notable books include: Songs from Dewachen and Yak Horns: Notes on Contemporary Tibetan Writing, Music, Film and Politics. He is a founding member of TibetWrites and its publishing imprint, Blackneck Books. His essays, commentaries, and literary writings have appeared in prominent international publications including the Washington Post, Asahi Weekly, the Journal of Indian Literature, and HIMAL Southasian, among others.
